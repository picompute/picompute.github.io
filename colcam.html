HOW IT WORKS

Start with a photo

Colcam is designed around a simple idea: use your phone's camera to capture the things you want to keep track of.

1

Capture

Take a photo of an item in your collection using your phone's camera.

2

Identify

Colcam can recognize text and identify possible objects in your photo to help describe the item.

3

Organize

Save the item, add useful information, and search your collection whenever you need it.

COLLECTION DETAILS

Keep the information that matters

A photo provides the starting point. Colcam also lets you record additional information about each item.

Keep the details you need without having to maintain a separate spreadsheet or handwritten list.

Purchase Date Record when you acquired the item.
Price Keep track of the item's purchase price.
Quantity Record how many items you have.
Location Keep track of where an item is stored.
Notes Add additional information about your item.
FIND YOUR ITEMS

Turn your photos into searchable information

Recognized text can become part of your item's description. This makes it possible to search and filter your collection using meaningful words found in your photos.

Search by what you see

Instead of remembering exactly where an item was stored, use information associated with the item to help find it in your collection.
