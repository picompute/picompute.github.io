HOW COLCAM WORKS

Start with a photo

Colcam is designed around a simple idea: use your phone's camera to capture the things you want to keep track of.

1

Capture

Take a photo of an item in your collection using your phone's camera.

2

Identify

Colcam can recognize text and suggest possible object names for items in your photos.

3

Organize

Save the item, add useful information, and search your collection when you need it.

ITEM INFORMATION

Keep the details that matter

A photo provides the starting point. Colcam also lets you record additional information about each item.

Keep the details you need to understand, manage, and find your collection later.

Purchase Date Record when you acquired the item.
Price Keep track of the item's purchase price.
Quantity Record how many items you have.
Location Keep track of where an item is stored.
Notes Add additional information about your item.
SEARCH YOUR COLLECTION

Find items using information in your photos

Recognized text can become part of your item's description, making your collection easier to search and filter using meaningful words found in your photos.

Search by what you see

Instead of remembering exactly where an item was stored, search using information associated with the item.
