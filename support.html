Colcam Support

Welcome to Colcam Support. We are here to help you use Colcam to organize and manage your personal collections through images.

About Colcam

Colcam helps you build and manage image-based collections. Depending on the features available in your app version, you can identify items using suggested object names, organize photos, search descriptions, and record details such as purchase date, price, quantity, location, and notes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I organize an item?

Take or add a photo using the app's available photo workflow, then follow the on-screen instructions to identify and organize the item.

What if the suggested object name is incorrect?

Review the available suggestions and select the most suitable name. If the app offers previously recognized names, you may be able to select one of those instead.

Can I record additional information about an item?

Colcam supports additional collection details such as purchase date, price, quantity, location, and notes.

How do I report a problem or request a feature?

Contact our support team using the email address below. Please describe the issue and the steps needed to reproduce it.
