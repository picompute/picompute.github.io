Privacy Policy for Colcam

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Perspective Intelligence ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Colcam mobile application ("Colcam"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Colcam.

1. Camera and Photos

Colcam uses your device's camera to allow you to capture photographs. Colcam may also allow you to select photographs from your device, depending on the features available in the application. Camera and photo access is requested only when needed for these features.

2. Text Recognition

Colcam processes photographs to recognize text contained in the images. The recognized text may be associated with the corresponding photograph so that you can search for and organize your captured information.

3. Information Stored by the App

Colcam may store photographs and recognized text on your device as part of the application's functionality.

You should not use Colcam to capture sensitive or confidential information unless you are comfortable with how that information is handled by the application.

4. Information We Collect

Perspective Intelligence does not intentionally collect personal information from you through Colcam except where such collection is specifically described in the application or required to provide a particular service.

If we introduce analytics, advertising, cloud services, or other third-party services that collect information, this Privacy Policy may be updated to describe those services and the information they collect.

5. Advertising

Colcam may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob.

If advertising is introduced, those third-party services may collect or receive certain information from your device in accordance with their own privacy policies.

This Privacy Policy will be updated with additional details regarding advertising and data practices when applicable.

6. Third-Party Services

Colcam may use third-party services to provide certain functionality. Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Children's Privacy

Colcam is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
